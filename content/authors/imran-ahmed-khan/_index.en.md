# About Me

I completed the Master of Informatics program at Shizuoka University's Graduate School of Integrated Science and Technology in September 2026, where I conducted research as a member of Ishikawa Lab. My work focuses on multimodal emotion recognition, particularly the extent to which EEG-based models can generalize to people who were not represented during training. I am interested in the gap between benchmark performance and dependable use in human-centered affective computing.

Before joining Shizuoka University, I earned a B.Tech. in Information Technology from Lovely Professional University and worked in software engineering. I draw on both backgrounds by combining machine-learning experiments with an emphasis on reproducible evaluation and practical deployment.

On 20 March 2026, I presented **“Deployment Limits of Cross-Subject EEG Emotion Recognition: A Multimodal Baseline Study”**, coauthored with Shogo Ishikawa, at the **1st International Workshop on Human-centred Emotion-Awareness and Sensing in Pervasive Computing (EmotionSense 2026)**. The workshop was held in conjunction with the **24th IEEE International Conference on Pervasive Computing and Communications (IEEE PerCom 2026)** in Pisa, Italy. The paper appears in the *2026 IEEE PerCom Workshops and Other Affiliated Events* proceedings, pages 1–5 ([DOI: 10.1109/PERCOMWORKSHOPS68308.2026.11585457](https://doi.org/10.1109/PERCOMWORKSHOPS68308.2026.11585457)).
